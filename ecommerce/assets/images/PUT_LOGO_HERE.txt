Save your transparent-background logo here as exactly:

    logo.png

Steps to make your logo transparent:
1. Go to https://www.remove.bg/upload
2. Upload the "On The Line" logo JPG
3. Download the transparent PNG
4. Rename it to logo.png
5. Save it inside this folder (assets/images/logo.png)

The page will automatically:
  - add a white border around it
  - add an orange glow
  - show it BIG on the homepage hero
  - shrink it into the top-left corner as you scroll

If logo.png is missing, a fallback "OL" badge shows up automatically.
